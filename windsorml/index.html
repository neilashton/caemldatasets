01 · Overview

What is WindsorML?

WindsorML contains 355 geometric variants of the Windsor body, each evaluated with a GPU-native, wall-modelled large-eddy simulation. The dataset occupies the useful middle ground between a simplified Ahmed body and a full road-car configuration.

The simulations use a Cartesian immersed-boundary approach with roughly 300 million cells per case. Geometry, three-dimensional surface and volume fields, force and moment coefficients and rendered views are supplied in interoperable formats for machine-learning research.
