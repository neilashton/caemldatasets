01 · Overview

What is AhmedML?

AhmedML contains 500 parametrically varied Ahmed bodies simulated with a time-accurate, scale-resolving CFD workflow. The simplified geometry retains important road-vehicle flow physics—including pressure-induced separation and three-dimensional vortical structures—without the geometric complexity of a full vehicle.

Every case includes geometry, surface and volume fields, force and moment coefficients, slices and rendered images in open formats. The OpenFOAM setup and validation cases are also available, making the dataset useful for surrogate modelling, geometric learning, reduced-order modelling and reproducibility studies.
