Official AhmedML train, validation and test splits are now available. The release defines eight deterministic benchmark regimes: a random baseline, three nested data-efficiency subsets, and four out-of-distribution evaluations based on geometry, drag and image-derived wake structure. Download the [JSON manifest](/assets/downloads/ahmedml/ahmedml_official_splits/splits/manifest.json), read the [methodology](/assets/downloads/ahmedml/ahmedml_official_splits/docs/README.pdf), or obtain the [complete reproducibility package](/assets/downloads/ahmedml/ahmedml_official_splits.zip). Partition sizes and a usage example are included in the [AhmedML dataset record](/ahmedml/#official-splits).
