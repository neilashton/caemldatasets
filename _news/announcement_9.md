Official WindsorML train, validation and test splits are now available. The release defines eight deterministic benchmark regimes: an approximately 80/10/10 random baseline, three nested data-efficiency subsets, and four out-of-distribution evaluations based on geometry, drag and image-derived wake structure. Download the [JSON manifest](/assets/downloads/windsorml/windsorml_official_splits/splits/manifest.json), read the [methodology](/assets/downloads/windsorml/windsorml_official_splits/docs/README.pdf), or obtain the [complete reproducibility package](/assets/downloads/windsorml/windsorml_official_splits.zip). Partition sizes, provenance and a usage example are included in the [WindsorML dataset record](/windsorml/#official-splits).
