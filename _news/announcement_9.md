Official WindsorML train, validation and test splits are now available. The release defines eight deterministic benchmark regimes: an approximately 80/10/10 random baseline, three nested data-efficiency subsets, and four out-of-distribution evaluations based on geometry, drag and image-derived wake structure. Download the [JSON manifest](https://huggingface.co/datasets/neashton/windsorml/resolve/main/splits/manifest.json), read the [methodology](https://huggingface.co/datasets/neashton/windsorml/resolve/main/splits/README.pdf), or browse the [complete reproducibility files on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/neashton/windsorml/tree/main/splits). Partition sizes, provenance and a usage example are included in the [WindsorML dataset record](/windsorml/#official-splits).
