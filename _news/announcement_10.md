Mesh-native surface-area sidecars are now available for [AhmedML](/ahmedml/#surface-quadrature), [WindsorML](/windsorml/#surface-quadrature), [DrivAerML](/drivaerml/#surface-quadrature) and [HiLiftAeroML](/hiliftaeroml/#surface-quadrature). AhmedML and DrivAerML provide polygon-cell areas aligned with native boundary CellData; WindsorML and HiLiftAeroML provide barycentric dual-area weights aligned with native boundary points. Each dataset record documents coverage, units, mesh compatibility and provenance, and its download selector can retrieve the NPY sidecars with the corresponding manifest and validation records.
