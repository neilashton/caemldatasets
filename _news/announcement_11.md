[DrivAerML](/drivaerml/) now includes STEP CAD geometry for all 484 publicly available runs. The files use millimetres and are stored as `run_N/drivaer_N.step` alongside the existing STL geometry and CFD data. Browse the [Hugging Face repository](https://huggingface.co/datasets/neashton/drivaerml) or choose **CAD geometry (STEP)** in the [download selector](/drivaerml/#download) to retrieve the CAD files, totalling approximately 149 GB.
